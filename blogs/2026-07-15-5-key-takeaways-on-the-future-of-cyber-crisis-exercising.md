Most organizations recognize that cyber threats are inevitable. What separates leaders from laggards is not whether they experience disruption, but how effectively they respond when it happens. Cyber resilience has moved beyond prevention - it’s now about maintaining continuity, making confident decisions under pressure, and recovering quickly in the face of uncertainty.
